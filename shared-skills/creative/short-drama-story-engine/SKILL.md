# Short Drama Story Engine

## Overview

This skill governs the **story and screenplay layer** for爽文、爽剧、真人微短剧、AI漫剧、动态漫和一人分饰多角系列内容. It stops before production-level shot design and model prompting.

The working premise is:

> A high-retention story identifies an audience's unresolved emotional debt, makes that debt concrete through character conflict, and repays it through a causal, costly, witnessed choice.

“情绪债”和本技能中的爽感公式是创作工作模型，不冒充学术定律。Use them to force clear decisions, not to replace research, taste, or character truth.

## When to Use

Load this skill when the user asks to:

- 写爽剧、短剧、AI漫剧、动态漫或系列剧情；
- 设计整季大纲、分集结构、钩子、反转、付费卡点；
- 检查“为什么不爽、为什么不追、为什么人物很假”；
- 处理寻亲、真假身份、窃稿、复仇、逆袭、重生、隐藏身份等多线故事；
- 把世界观角色改编成独立演员作品；
- 在正式拆镜前锁定故事范围与AI生产难度。

Do not use this skill for:

- 已确认剧本的镜头拆分：转 `shot-language`；
- 单纯生成图片或视频提示词：转对应模型技能；
- 世界观资料管理：转 `world-building`；
- 角色Vlog文案拆镜：转 `character-vlog-pipeline`。

## Twelve-Animal / Multi-Character Cultural IPs

Use this section for Chinese zodiac stories and other culturally anchored ensemble IPs with 8–12 symbolic characters.

Do not default to a retelling of the zodiac race or assign fixed personality traits as if they were cultural facts. Treat each animal as a **symbol under pressure**, not a horoscope label.

### Framework

1. **One anchor protagonist** carries the audience through the ensemble and the central mystery; twelve equal protagonists usually dissolve the plot.
2. Give each animal four fields: inherited stereotype, recognizable emotional debt, counter-choice, and irreversible cost.
3. Group the twelve into 3–4 relationship clusters by dramatic function (survival, misrecognition, expectation, belonging, etc.), not merely by traditional order.
4. Create one recurring mechanism that ties every unit to the season question. Each episode must repay or materially advance one existing debt while moving that mechanism forward.
5. Make the central conflict about agency, not just defeating a villain: e.g. characters resisting being reduced to what others say their zodiac means.

### Animal-to-Character Action-Law Audit

Do not stop at a zodiac label, occupation, or virtue word such as “hardworking ox,” “clever monkey,” “loyal dog,” or “wise leader.” Those create functional job descriptions, not characters. For every animal protagonist, derive one **pressure reflex**: the visible action they perform before they have time to explain themselves.

Keep three layers explicitly separate:

1. **Animal observation:** a narrowly stated, source-checkable behavioral trait; do not universalize one species’ behavior to every animal in a broad category.
2. **Public/cultural image:** the audience’s familiar stereotype; label it as cultural material, not biology.
3. **Original dramatic translation:** the character’s unique work style and moral blind spot in this story.

Build a five-part action card before writing the character’s entrance scene:

| Field | Required question |
|---|---|
| First question | What does this person instinctively ask when danger appears? |
| First action | What physically/socially visible move do they make before explaining? |
| Expression pattern | How does that reflex sound in dialogue: pacing, sentence type, vocabulary, silence? |
| Plausible misreading | What do others reasonably think that visible action means? |
| Backfire and correction | How does the strength cross another person’s boundary, and what does growth retain rather than erase? |

Use an **action-geometry test**: put all protagonists before the same obstacle. If two would take the same first action, one is still a label and must be redesigned. A useful contrast is not that each person says a different opinion, but that their actions obstruct and rescue one another.

For a character whose animal image implies hidden or indirect survival, do not reduce the trait to “they are misunderstood.” Write a causal chain: **ambiguous visible action → reasonable suspicion → concrete outcome → retrospective understanding**. The outcome may validate the motive but must not erase the trust cost or excuse harm done by withholding other people’s right to choose risk.

For OPC one-click production, do not pause for approval between profile design and script generation. Internally audit the action-law direction against established season causality, repair only contradictions that can be resolved without changing locked facts, and carry the resulting character design into the script. For ordinary conversational drafting outside OPC, preserve the user's explicit review preference. See `references/animal-action-law-audit.md` for a reusable audit grid and the five-character workplace example; see `references/animal-action-law-to-episode-chain.md` for the approved-character-card → season-causality → first-episode-action-chain workflow, including the hidden-action misreading pattern.

### Modern Workplace Water-Margin Ensemble

Use this section when a Chinese zodiac or multi-animal ensemble is relocated into modern employment, entrepreneurship, public-sector, large-company, or private-enterprise settings and uses a Water Margin-like gathering arc.

#### Slow-gathering rule: relationships are produced by consequences

For a Water Margin-style ensemble, do not begin with a preassembled team, fixed relationship map, assigned trio structure, or shared project that explains why all protagonists know one another. Start each core character in an independent life and let the cast expand through causally connected accidents:

> one person → two people → four people → a growing circle → the core ensemble.

“Accidental” meetings must still have an antecedent. A new character may be drawn in by the previous character’s choice, mistake, debt, evidence, resource, client, conflict, reputation, or unfinished consequence. Time and geography may be asynchronous. Do not force all characters into one calendar, workplace, city, or initial crisis. The shared venture is an outcome after the group has substantially formed, not the premise that introduces everyone.

Before drawing a relationship map, create independent life cards. Each card records: subjective success, personal pride, the narrow world-model produced by that success, the way the strength backfires, the life lesson that exposes its boundary, the ability worth retaining, and the direction the character chooses for themself. Only after these cards are approved should encounter chains be derived from cause and effect.

#### Subjective success is not a ranking

Do not define “success” on one axis such as money, title, scale, education, fame, or competence. Success is what the character sincerely regards as a hard-won achievement and source of pride. It may be keeping a family intact, preserving a craft, surviving with dignity, protecting someone, earning trust, finishing a small business, holding a principle, finding a new route, or leading a major organization. The story must not invalidate these achievements merely because they are small or private.

The dramatic problem is that a formerly successful method can become a false universal law:

> past success → justified pride → simplified explanation of the world → strength becomes rigidity → consequence teaches a boundary → ability is retained, worldview is widened.

The lesson is not “the character was always wrong.” The character must keep the useful ability and personally choose a broader direction. Avoid turning growth into obedience, personality erasure, or promotion into a supposedly higher human value.

#### Institutional guide / common-direction figure

If the ensemble includes a symbolic national or institutional guide, do not make the figure an omniscient savior who summons the cast, assigns everyone a fixed role, or solves their decisive problems. Dramatize leadership through recognizing differences, creating a fair platform, setting boundaries, mediating conflict, distributing opportunity, accepting feedback, and carrying long-term responsibility. The characters must make the decisive choices themselves.

A panda-like figure may symbolize effective national leadership: it helps diverse people find a workable common direction without making them identical. Give this figure a leadership risk—such as overlooking concrete people in the name of the larger goal—and require correction through feedback and accountability. “Common direction” is not a fixed job allocation; it is a condition in which different abilities can remain distinct and cooperate.

Add a reference pointer: see `references/slow-gathering-ensemble.md` for the reusable independent-life-card schema and encounter-causality audit.

The core translation is not “twelve people cooperate at work.” It is:

> Individuals scattered across different organizations are pushed out of their original positions, meet through overlapping interests, form a risky mutual-protection network, build a shared venture, gain influence, face recruitment or incorporation by a larger system, and must decide what kind of community they are willing to sustain.

Lock the causal chain before episode count:

1. **Separated work worlds:** place characters in materially different systems—large company, private firm, independent entrepreneurship, public-sector or institutional employment, freelance/outsourced work, and small-business or service work. Do not make occupation a fixed animal trait; occupation is the pressure environment, while the animal is a symbolic survival style.
2. **Individual displacement debts:** each character needs a distinct workplace wound: stolen credit, scapegoating, exclusion by credential or region, rule-bound sacrifice, debt, non-compete pressure, ethical retaliation, resource capture, or a self-caused failure. Some characters must be both harmed and culpable.
3. **Encounter through a shared dependency:** the first meeting should be caused by a project, client, technology, supply chain, evidence trail, or market failure that no one character controls. The first interaction is suspicion or transactional cooperation, not instant fraternity.
4. **Mutual use before mutual protection:** let characters exchange leverage for short-term survival. The group becomes bound when abandoning one member exposes the others to credible, irreversible harm.
5. **Shared venture as the modern Liangshan:** the group needs a real operating business or platform, not only revenge or a protest alliance. Define its users, value exchange, revenue or resource loop, and why the twelve contributions are complementary. A useful prototype is a national collaboration service connecting ordinary people, small businesses, talent, technology, orders, and regional resources—but treat this as a creative option, not a default.
6. **Power growth creates a second oppression:** after success, the group must reproduce some of the gatekeeping, hierarchy, opacity, or exploitation it once opposed. This creates the internal fracture that replaces a simplistic final boss.
7. **Recruitment/incorporation choice:** a larger institution, capital network, platform, or public system offers legitimacy and scale. Split the group over autonomy, responsibility, distribution, and access to a larger common market—not over abstract slogans alone.
8. **Commonwealth endpoint:** “nation” or “country” should enter through shared rules, markets, risks, infrastructure, rights, responsibilities, and long-term future. The endpoint is not that everyone becomes identical; it is that different people accept a common table and common obligations while retaining disagreement.

### Historical Animal Allegory Guardrails

When the ensemble uses “other animals” to evoke historical rise, decline, invasion, absorption, lost surnames, or discontinued lineages:

- Treat the animal layer as an original symbolic taxonomy of survival strategies, organizations, inherited institutions, or cultural residues—not a one-to-one encoding of real ethnicities, nations, or contemporary groups.
- Distinguish documented history, later narrative tradition, creative inference, and original invention before making historical claims.
- Prefer the mechanisms of history—migration, competition, alliance, institutionalization, cultural fusion, renamed or absorbed lineages, adaptation, and loss of independent identity—over a crude bloodline “winner/loser” model.
- A defeated or discontinued animal character should still leave a technique, institution, memory, or ethical cost behind. “Removed from the final twelve” does not mean “meaningless.”
- Use the 108-hero echo as a scale and structural resonance, not as a requirement to introduce 108 equal protagonists in season one. Separate core figures, consequential secondary figures, and witnesses of historical change.
- Never turn “the nation” into a speech-only savior. Dramatize it through a decision involving common rules, shared risk, market access, public responsibility, or a future larger than the founders’ private interests.

A compact endpoint card is:

> separated organizations → distinct workplace debts → transactional encounter → forced mutual protection → shared venture → influence and institutional temptation → internal reproduction of old harms → incorporation/recruitment split → common rules and shared future → twelve remain as a coalition capable of continued cooperation, not as twelve biologically superior winners.

Do not finalize episode count until this card, the venture’s operating loop, the animal-allegory boundaries, and the internal fracture are stable.

### Ensemble Public-Task Endpoint

For a 8–12 character ensemble whose season ends with a shared public task, lock the endpoint before writing character biographies or episode indices. The task must be a causal system, not a generic group battle.

1. Define one public system or shared value that fails, its deadline, and the concrete loss ordinary people suffer if it remains broken.
2. Decompose the task into a causal network of indispensable bottlenecks. Do not merely assign one symbolic station to each animal; every bottleneck needs a prerequisite, a unique contribution, a failure consequence, and a physical or witness trace.
3. Preserve the “no prior communication” premise by giving each protagonist an independent local motive and partial view of the same crisis. Their first encounter should produce interference or accusation, not instant friendship.
4. Build the emotional debt into the task: at least one protagonist is misunderstood, one person's labor is buried, and an outside animal can claim the visible final step as a complete victory. The credit theft should exploit a believable record or visibility gap, not require everyone else to become stupid.
5. Use a false victory before the final settlement. The credit claimant can receive public recognition because the system appears repaired, but a second failure exposes that they know the visible endpoint rather than the full causal chain.
6. Make the protagonists choose to repair the public harm before defending their reputation. This choice establishes the team's shared value and separates them from the claimant.
7. Resolve the reputation debt through multiple independent evidence lines: material traces, time records, witnesses, unique tools, and causal reconstruction. A single confession or authority declaration underpays a social misrecognition.
8. End with public vindication that closes the season task but creates a justified next-season conflict: recognition, temporary status, or public support is not automatically the same as formal office. A later contest or institutional challenge should arise from the remaining fairness dispute.

A useful endpoint card is:

> public system → deadline → failure cost → indispensable bottlenecks → independent entry motives → misunderstanding/buried labor → visible credit theft → false victory → second failure → evidence chain → public settlement → next-season dispute.

Do not finalize episode count until this card and the bottleneck order are stable. For detailed use, see `references/ensemble-public-task-endpoint.md`.

### Modernizing a Cultural Anchor Without Flattening It

When the user wants to replace an ancient social motif with a modern equivalent, do not transplant the old surface object directly (for example, agriculture → a new magical calendar). First identify the invariant human problem underneath it.

1. **Find the invariant:** ask what ordinary people feared or depended on in the original society. For seasonal agriculture, the deeper invariant is livelihood, collective coordination, continuity, and whether a person can secure a place in the next cycle—not farming itself.
2. **Choose a modern thematic anchor:** for contemporary ensemble stories, a strong translation is **安身立命**: whether ordinary people can keep living, working, caring for family, and retaining dignity inside a changing city or system.
3. **Use three layers, not one abstract mechanism:**
   - theme: the human question, such as 安身立命 or opportunity/position;
   - public system: a legible modern dependency, such as a city's water, power, transport, communications, logistics, or care network;
   - season event: one concrete deadline-driven task, such as switching an old bridge and its surrounding life line before closure.
4. **Separate strategic direction from local implementation:** a national or regional goal may be rational at the long-term/system level while residents experience immediate costs. The conflict should be an information and translation gap, not a lazy “government villain versus people” binary.
5. **Give the deadline multiple causal clocks:** a synchronized regional project window, a physical safety/weather window, and a visible local closure or inspection date. Let characters discover why the date matters through evidence and consequences, not a lecture.
6. **Make participation improve the policy's landing:** protagonists should not merely submit to the plan or defeat it. They preserve the strategic objective while adding neglected routes for vulnerable residents, merchants, schools, clinics, transport, and memory.
7. **Modernization should not equal erasure:** if an old structure is no longer fit for its original load, adapt it into a public memory, pedestrian, cultural, or community function. This expresses continuity without blocking necessary development.
8. **Handle international comparisons carefully:** do not assert that a whole country or governance model stagnates for one simple reason. If contrast is needed, dramatize a fictional or local “deferred renewal” counterexample—years of patching, declining access, out-migration, and accumulated risk—so the audience infers the cost of inaction.
9. **For this user, framework first:** lock the invariant, public system, deadline, information gap, and season capacity before writing dialogue or a full episode index. When a framework is approved, proceed to the next layer in ordinary chat; do not reopen settled decisions through clarification cards.

See `references/modern-cultural-translation.md` for the reusable translation matrix, information-gap model, and season-capacity pattern.

### Cultural and audience-hook audit before locking the public task

Before naming an ensemble's central public system or presenting it as culturally grounded, classify every major term as one of:

1. documented historical/customary practice;
2. regional folklore or later popular retelling;
3. creative inference inspired by a real practice;
4. original invention for this fictional world.

Do not let a poetic or dictionary term silently become a fabricated historical institution. For example, a real word meaning the sequence of years/seasons does not by itself establish a historical mechanism, office, canal, ritual system, or twelve-node infrastructure. Label original mechanisms as original in the outline and never use source language that implies they are traditional fact.

Run the first-screen hook test:

- Can a new viewer tell in one scene what is failing?
- Can they see who will suffer if it is not fixed?
- Is the deadline concrete?
- Does the task create an immediate character choice rather than a lore lecture?

If an abstract system needs several episodes of explanation before the audience understands the loss, demote it to background vocabulary and replace the visible public task with a concrete, culturally legible problem. For a Chinese zodiac ensemble, real anchors such as seasonal markers, spring society, spring ploughing, irrigation, grain storage, flood control, migration, or community ritual may support the world; the specific mechanism and cast of characters can still be original, but that boundary must be explicit.

A strong endpoint therefore has two layers:

- **public layer:** an immediately legible task such as getting spring water to fields before planting;
- **thematic layer:** a poetic vocabulary such as the turning of the seasons, used to deepen the story without carrying all the causal machinery.

Do not lock episode count, title names, or character biographies around an abstract system until this audit passes. If research shows that the core mechanism is invented and the hook is weak, revise the public task first rather than defending the earlier invention.

### Gender Derivation for Animal-Ensemble Roles

When assigning gender to zodiac or animal-symbol characters, do **not** start from a target male/female ratio, visual pairing, or an attempt to reverse stereotypes for its own sake. Gender is a story consequence, not a roster-balancing device.

**Baseline-first rule (user-corrected, high priority):** default to the audience's stereotype (`顺应刻板印象`), and reverse only when the plot has a *non-reversible* strong reason. The trap this fixes: using "which gender version is more concrete / sharper" (`男女两版比谁更尖锐`) as the *sole* gate systematically flips traditional male-dominated roles to female — because in those fields the female version almost always gains an extra discrimination layer and therefore always looks "more concrete." That produces exactly the forced anti-stereotype reversal the user rejects. Consequence: traditional roles (现场带队扛活 / 技术写码 / 组织领导) default male unless the plot genuinely cannot work otherwise; relationship/service roles (客户维护 / 结算复核) may default female as the natural stereotype.

For each role, derive and present the decision in this order:

1. **Animal evidence:** identify only the sex-linked or behavioral facts that are genuinely relevant; do not invent biological claims or treat animal sex as automatic character destiny.
2. **Audience image:** state the Chinese public's familiar cultural and gender-coded impression separately from biological fact.
3. **Dramatic function:** test the role's pressure reflex, emotional debt, social cost, misreading, relationship position, and growth arc.
4. **Gender consequence:** explain exactly how male/female/kept-open makes the above conflict more concrete. If changing the gender changes nothing except surface appearance, do not lock it yet.

Keep these three layers visibly separate in the approval draft: **verified animal fact / public cultural image / original dramatic judgment**.

#### Workplace gender gate: lock before writing the episode

For a modern workplace ensemble, gender is not a late visual or casting field. It changes the power relationship through which a character's action is interpreted. Therefore, for every character who enters an episode, lock or explicitly keep open gender **before** treating the episode as a production-ready script.

Test the chosen gender against the specific workplace debt—not generic "men versus women" clichés:

- customer ownership, credit-taking, salary negotiation, promotion, networking, and private relationship leverage;
- who is presumed to absorb emotional labor, smooth over conflict, entertain clients, carry physical/risk-heavy work, or sign for responsibility;
- how a refusal is read (for example, as being difficult, not sociable, insufficiently committed, or avoiding responsibility);
- how the same visible action changes the plausible accusation, humiliation, retaliation, witness reaction, and later repayment;
- whether the character's correction can widen the workplace rule rather than merely reverse the gendered harm onto someone else.

A gender choice passes only when it makes the role's pressure reflex, misreading, emotional debt, social cost, and growth outcome more concrete — **but "more concrete" alone is not a sufficient gate**: in male-dominated fields the female version is almost always more concrete (extra discrimination layer), so concreteness as the default test flips every traditional role female. Start from the stereotype baseline; treat concreteness as the check you apply only when *justifying a reversal*. If changing gender changes only appearance, leave it open and do not write a supposedly final episode around it. Do not use sexual harassment, humiliation, or prefab gendered-office tropes as shortcut drama; the pressure must arise causally from this character's position, choice, and evidence chain.

For OPC one-click production, revise scripts, names/pronouns/honorifics, character profiles, visual assets, and voice design together inside the node pipeline; do not wait for user approval. For ordinary conversational drafting outside OPC, follow the user's explicit review preference. Remove accidental gendered wording from early scripts if the role is still open.

**Concrete cadence (one character at a time):** derive and present gender as a standalone approval draft per character — 鼠 → 牛 → 猴 → …, each gated by a separate "可以/继续". The decisive technique is to write out **both versions concretely** (女版推演 vs 男版推演) and lock only when one version produces a structurally different power consequence, not merely a re-worded accusation. When several consecutive roles derive to the same gender, stop and check *both* directions: it may be a natural per-plot result, **or** it may be the "sharper-version" trap silently flipping every male-dominated role female (user caught this when 鼠/牛/猴 all came out female). If the latter, reset to the stereotype baseline and re-derive rather than accepting the streak. See `references/gender-derivation-approval-draft.md` for the reusable draft skeleton and the 鼠/牛/猴 worked examples.

For the fantasy/zodiac-superhero genre playbook, see `references/xianxia-awakening-ensemble.md` (觉醒翻盘流、动物特征=异类=反差、美强惨、时间权柄体系、未来道具时效性、十二生肖历史考证、逻辑自洽教训). For quantified hook/rhythm rules (黄金3秒、憋屈≤30秒、落差即流量上限、信息差提前给观众、付费卡点、诊断"平/闷"七问), see `references/hook-rhythm-rules.md`.

### Mythic Reincarnation & Future-Prop Cheat (玄幻神转世 + 未来道具)

When the Chinese-zodiac IP is reworked into a xianxia/mythic "twelve hour-gods banished to the mortal world" premise:

- **Protagonist MUST be the zodiac god itself** (神转世成凡人), never a "mortal host who binds a god" (凡人宿主). User corrected this explicitly — "角色还得是让12生肖是主角，跑偏了". The hook is "gods reincarnated as outcasts, each bearing one animal mark that mortals read as monstrous / as a curse"; a mortal-host rewrite loses the per-animal identity and the whole IP's coverage.
- **Future-prop mechanic (哆啦A梦式降维打击)**: the dragon (ruler of 时流/time-flow) reaches through time to borrow modern objects — lighter, flashlight, telescope, compass — and hands them to an awakened god to crush ancient-era problems. **Hard rule — future objects are time-limited**: they vanish on schedule and auto-return to their own era, doubled as a running gag cutting 1-2s to a modern person ("咦？我打火机呢？"). Props must be VARIED, not one recurring item — user found a single lighter "太没意思了".
- **Do NOT over-engineer the cost rule.** User rejected "the more futuristic the prop, the shorter its duration / the higher its cost" as wrong ("这个设定不对吧"). The clean rule: future objects are time-limited and auto-return; natural scarcity = "borrowing drains the dragon's strength, so he can't borrow often." Adding layered cost rules created a logic hole ("why does the fire stay behind after the lighter vanishes?") that had to be walked back across three files.
- **Paradox deadlock as a mystery**: "gather all twelve gods to free the dragon, but the dragon is one of the twelve" is self-defeating. Make it intentional — the villain (猫神) engineered the deadlock, and the dragon KNOWS the answer but is cut off mid-sentence every time he tries to say it ("缺的那一个，其实——"). Stretch that half-sentence across a full volume as the through-line suspense.
- **Gender-emotion engine (ancient-mythic flavor)**: female gods = 险境+觉醒反杀 (forced marriage / being sold / near-assault); male gods = 苦工/充军/奴役. Assault threats MUST stop at "险境" and flip to awakening-counterattack — writing the actual assault turns it into 虐女, repels the female-paying audience (55.8%), and fails review. This is the same "险些 is the switch" rule as the modern-workplace case, but the pressure sources differ.
- **Self-review against chapter one as the intensity baseline** (user's standing instruction — "从第一卷开始检查，对比第一章的精彩程度"): compare every later chapter to the opening's punch. Watch for (a) "remembered awakening" — a god whose awakening happened in backstory and is only narrated, not shown live (must be re-shot as an on-screen burst, and it often hides a logic hole like "he already awakened, so why is he still locked up?"); (b) flat transition/info chapters that lack a 打脸 beat; (c) awakening-ordering bugs (编号 "第二个/第三个" repeated or wrong), gender-pronoun drift, and miscounts in the god-roster (亮/暗/空 counts).

### Cultural and production gates

- Before fixing lore, separate documented custom, regional folklore, later popular retelling, creative inference, and original invention; research authoritative and local sources before making cultural claims.
- Do not use a single internet personality list as the basis for all twelve characters.
- For AI animation, keep most scenes to one or two principal characters, give each animal a stable visual/action anchor, and avoid relying on twelve-person group performance for emotional clarity.
- Before character design, image generation, storyboarding, or voice casting, build an authoritative **identity crosswalk** from the completed prose itself: sequence/time-slot, symbolic deity, prose character name, gender/form, stable visual anchor, ability/action anchor, and evidence status. Do not infer identity from eye color alone, and do not silently merge an early teaser with a contradictory formal appearance.
- Treat characters introduced in a batch, lacking a mortal name, independent awakening scene, or persistent silhouette/garment/prop marker as a visual-recognition risk. Report the gap honestly before inventing new traits. Use `references/ensemble-identity-visual-audit.md` for the table, evidence rules, and weak-recognition audit.
- Lock audience, medium, season length, standalone-versus-connected structure, and source boundaries before writing episode dialogue.

A useful working question is: “If a person is defined by one sentence about their zodiac, what choice proves they are more than that sentence?”

## 上瘾核心与节奏铁律（爽文/短剧）

> 上瘾的核心不是"爽"，是"对下一个爽点的期待"。爽文 vs 普通故事的本质区别：情绪账本**高频结算**（每章/每集至少一次"压抑→释放"闭环）vs 低频、长周期、延迟结算。诊断"太平太闷" = 三病并发：冲突前置不够、爽点密度太低、集末无钩子。

节奏硬数字（行业规律，已交叉验证，详见 `references/爽文上瘾核心与节奏铁律.md`）：

- 前3秒必须出冲突（钩子）、5秒给承诺；开场铺垫必死。爆款开场最高转化是"被公开羞辱/赶出家门"（78%）。
- 憋屈不超过30秒，受辱后马上反击；不能跨多集只受辱不改变。
- 每集至少1个"打脸"闭环（受辱→反击→当众打脸）；**连续两集无反转 = 硬伤**。
- 单集密度：每15-30秒一个信息点/小反转，每集≥1个反转，最后15秒留新悬念。
- 打脸四步法：羞辱40% + 反击30% + 释放30%；反派话越狠，打脸越爽。
- 信息差要**提前给观众**（观众知道主角有底牌、反派不知道），享受"我早知道你死定了"的优越感——比捂到最后当反转爽得多。
- 爽点逐级变大（平级→主管→总监→老板），不能每次一样大。
- 付费卡点：6-10集免费，卡在"身份揭露/第一次大打脸"前一刻，不卡在爽完之后。

### 开场结构：好莱坞五种黄金开场（用户指定学习，2026-09-21）

前3秒出冲突是**密度**要求；开场**做什么**另有五种结构可选：

1. **冲突驱动型**——动作进行时＋危机已爆发＋结果悬置（不铺垫、不交代背景，第一秒进冲突）；
2. **视觉奇观型**——不可能的物理现象＋日常场景基底＋**平静的观察者**（观察者越平静，奇观越震撼）；
3. **人物塑造型**——极端特写局部＋标志性动作＋声音先入，**延迟识别**（高级的人物开场先不拍脸）；
4. **世界观震撼型**——极端大远景＋渺小参照物＋一个反常细节（建立镜头升级：交代"你从未见过的世界"）；
5. **悬念问题型**——先给结果＋露出不合理的细节＋答案在后（认知闭合需求，克制留白）。

五种**可组合使用**（视觉奇观＋悬念、人物特写＋冲突爆发）。**冲突型开场忌大全景**——多用中近景/特写/低机位仰拍。

### 反差放大铁律（AI漫剧/玄幻IP，详见 `references/反差吸引流量方法论.md`）

反差 = 打破刻板印象 → 认知失调 → 情绪共鸣。**落差即流量上限**——反差天花板 = 两端拉得有多开。

- 神写成"天道支柱"级（开天辟地镇守天道根基），凡人写成"被全世界抛弃、连蝼蚁都不如"，中间落差才是流量。
- 连环反转（反差的反差）：铺垫A（废物）→反转B（觉醒露一手）→再反转C（那只是神格一丝残影，真相更深）。观众以为猜到结局，其实才到半程。
- 多重反差叠加：场景+穷富+认知+身份4层叠满，每多一层爽点指数级放大。
- 每个生肖神绑定专属反差维度（牛=憨厚×巨力、蛇=柔媚×冷酷、龙=落魄×王霸…），避免12个神一个套路。

### 美强惨与玄幻化（视觉拉满·用户定调）

**美强惨 = 最强爽点放大器**（用户明确要求"每一个角色必须都是个顶个的颜值担当"）。三要素联动：美（观众第一眼沦陷、产生保护欲）→ 惨（这么美的人被欺负，观众心疼+愤怒值拉满）→ 强（觉醒打脸，前面积累的心疼愤怒瞬间兑换成爽感）。"这么好看的人，你们怎么下得去手"就是观众追剧的全部动力。

- **越美越异类**：封闭环境里"异于常人"本身是原罪。绝美的人长红瞳/竖瞳/额间金纹，凡人不觉得好看，只觉得"狐狸精转世"。美，成了被伤害的帮凶——这是最深的讽刺，也是最狠的情绪债。
- **落魄美**：破衣烂衫裹绝世容颜，"美人落难却难掩绝色"本身是最强视觉反差。
- **神级颜值 = 神转世的第一个伏笔**：AI漫剧是唯一能做到"神级颜值"的媒介（真人演员颜值有上限）。观众第一眼看到"美到不像凡人"的脸，不用台词就埋好了"他不是凡人"。
- **动物特征做"妖异的美"，不做丑**：动物印记（红瞳、竖瞳、额纹、鳞纹、角状发旋）要设计成惊艳而非怪异——角色"怪得合理、美得惊心动魄"，异类但不丑（太怪物化会破坏代入）。

**玄幻化 = 给爽点松绑**：现实题材会自我束缚"逆袭合不合理""信息差从哪来"，神话体系里"被封印的神觉醒"天然合理，爽点可以无限拉满。这就是把现实中做不到的脑洞合理化的路径，也是AI漫剧（低成本炫酷视觉）的天然主场。

**三层真相（被贬/被冤枉类主链结构）**：主链真相不要一句话讲完，做成连环反转、逐层放大——第一层"表面说法"（失序/犯错被贬）→ 第二层"被陷害"（编外复仇者/落选者，如"猫神"——借"猫落选生肖"这类人人皆知的梗反转成千年复仇）→ 第三层"真正的黑手"（窃天者/更高存在）。每一层都比上一层大，观众以为猜到结局其实才到半程；复仇对象也逐级变大（打棋子→打黑手）。

**金手指要有副作用（时间税）**：无敌会杀死爽点（没有憋屈就没有爽点）。给力量一个代价——用一次老一岁/丢记忆/神格磨损/寿数减一分。观众既盼主角出手，又替他捏汗。

### Ensemble 主角不可降级（用户纠正·高优先）

十二生肖/多角色 ensemble 当主体时，**主角必须就是这些生肖角色本身**（如"神转世成凡人"），不能降级成"凡人宿主绑定生肖神力量"的金手指结构。用户原话："十二生肖是主体，拆分谁都不好，像足球队""角色还得是让12生肖是主角，跑偏了"。原因是十二生肖覆盖所有观众、冷落哪个生肖=放弃那个生肖的观众；把生肖降级成"外挂"会丢掉这份主体性。要满足"白日做梦代入感"，在"神转世成凡人"框架内做（神转世本身就是隐藏身份+扫地僧+实力反差+扮猪吃虎四合一），不换主角。接力主角制（足球逻辑：每人轮流主场、每集有个人高光+团队推进）才是正确结构，不是平均分戏。

## Core Model: Emotional Debt

“受辱”本身不是爽点，它是在借债；行动与反击是在还债；公开、不可逆、有代价的反击是在连本带利还债。

常见债务类型：

- 尊严债：被轻视、羞辱、物化；
- 公平债：成果被抢、规则偏袒、劳动无偿；
- 身份债：被否认、被替代、真实身份被遮蔽；
- 爱情债：不被坚定选择、付出被利用；
- 认可债：能力被压制、长期不被看见；
- 亲情债：遗弃、控制、错认、隐瞒；
- 机会债：名额、资源、人生路径被夺走；
- 控制感债：人生长期由别人替自己决定。

Before plotting, answer:

1. 这部作品写给谁？
2. 他们最熟悉、最难说出口的委屈是什么？
3. 谁或什么规则制造了这笔债？
4. 主角替他们完成什么现实中不敢、不能或来不及完成的选择？
5. 这笔债如何具体、公开、不可逆地结算？

If question 5 has no concrete answer, do not start episode writing.

Detailed debt types, psychology cautions, and the working formula are in `references/emotional-debt-engine.md`.

Quantitative hook/pacing numbers (黄金3秒, 爽点密度, 打脸四步法, 付费卡点, 黄金三章, 网文爽点周期), the 反差 (contrast) engine, addiction psychology, and the 美强惨 amplifier are in `references/addiction-mechanics-data.md`. Consult it before fixing episode pacing or diagnosing "太平太闷" — do not decide rhythm by feel.

### 对白型定调与节奏口径（用户 2026-09 定，做漫剧/红果集剧本必须遵守）

**只做对白型，不做旁白型**：信息 100% 由角色之口说出，画面只负责奇观与情绪。

- **禁解说旁白（VO）**：禁第三人称叙述、"话说/原来"式解说、小说叙述句进剧本。设定/关系/前情/阴谋/任务/规则，每一项都要设计成"谁、在什么情境、对谁说出来"；无人说出的关键信息 = 漏做。角色传音/"声音"按对白处理，不算旁白。
- **OS 严格限量**：仅情绪峰值（每集 ≤2 处），只表达情绪、不承载新信息。
- **△ 降级为奇观层**：只写动作奇观、走位衔接、情绪外化；**△ 不得承载新信息**。
- **节奏用时间口径，不设字数硬限**（用户明确否掉"单句 ≤15 字"）：每 3 秒一句台词、单次发言 ≤2 句；句子长短服从口语听感，长句要念得顺、不拗口，禁为凑字数硬切碎。
- **每集有效台词 300–350 字**（收束集/过渡集同样不得缩水）；语音覆盖目标 ≥90%，无纯画面叙事段。
- **关键信息段（传音通告/身世自述/规则发布/死结分析）必须拆成多角色一问一答**，禁一人连说 3 句以上——信息在台词里但写成"独白播报"，听感仍是单声道。

实测基线（拉片 2 部红果爆款漫剧、4 样本 52 分钟 ASR）：语音覆盖 93.2%、每 3.2 秒一句、177 字/分钟、单句均 12.3 字（20.8% 超 15 字 → 15 字不是行业硬线）。详见 `references/hollywood-openings.md` 第三节。

### Continuous Causal Units Before Episode Count

For a long-form ensemble, especially a slow-gathering modern Water-Margin structure, do not jump directly from a season premise or a list of narrative blocks to an episode index. First convert the blocks into **stretchable continuous causal units**. A unit is not an episode and must not be forced into a one-unit/one-episode mapping.

Each unit should record:

1. **Opening state:** what the characters, affected public, team, and institution currently face;
2. **Primary debt:** which emotional or material debt is being advanced or partially repaid;
3. **Evidence action:** what evidence appears, changes hands, is reinterpreted, or enters a formal process;
4. **Character choice:** which character makes a decision only they can make;
5. **Visible result:** a concrete change in knowledge, risk, evidence, relationship, resource, status, or choice;
6. **Missing answer at the end:** one specific question that motivates the next unit.

A narrative block may contain multiple units, and the most important choice—such as collective self-exposure, a public remedy, or the formation of a shared mechanism—should not be compressed into a single meeting merely to fit a presumed episode count.

For public-task ensemble stories, place at least three visible clocks across the unit chain:

- a **public-user clock** showing when ordinary people lose access, income, safety, or continuity;
- a **frontline-survival clock** showing wages, maintenance, debt, equipment, credit, or eligibility worsening under delay;
- a **review/legitimacy clock** showing deadlines, evidence windows, appeal conditions, or institutional consequences.

Each clock should have a first countdown, a local relief, and a reverse acceleration. This creates rhythm without relying on arbitrary twists.

Before episode estimation, run a three-unit continuity check: within any three consecutive units, at least one of knowledge, risk, evidence, credit, resource, relationship, or agency must materially change. If a unit only repeats an argument, changes location, or restates known information, merge or rewrite it.

The capacity sequence is:

> season endpoint → information/evidence audit → stretchable causal units → unit rhythm cards → production-capacity estimate → provisional episode/season structure.

Never reverse this order by fitting unresolved causality into an inherited episode template. See `references/continuous-causal-units.md` for the reusable unit schema and clock audit.

### Four-Dimensional Audit Before Capacity Estimation

After continuous causal units, rhythm cards, and internal scene sequences are stable—but before estimating production capacity—build a **character–evidence–debt–witness** audit.

1. Test each major character's irreplaceability: remove them; if another person can perform the same decisive choice without changing causality, the character is only a function label.
2. Build an evidence ledger: first holder, transfer reason and cost, what each line proves, what it cannot prove, and when it formally enters.
3. Build a repayment ledger: distinguish borrowed debt, escalation, partial repayment, public staged settlement, permanent change, and new debt. A collective submission must not become collective exoneration.
4. Audit witness scope: personal recognition, transactional proof, relationship proof, formal review, organizational accountability, public consequence, and mechanism-level trace are different levels.
5. Identify continuous functional viewpoints for affected ordinary users, frontline workers, and the organization’s risk/closure position. They need not become named leads, but they cannot remain an abstract crowd if public debt is central.
6. Protect high-capacity turns from meeting compression: false victory and formal reversal; separately costly self-exposures; external questioning and responsibility splitting; staged public remedy; and a newly formed mechanism immediately facing a new exclusion.

Only after this audit passes may capacity estimation begin. See `references/four-dimensional-story-audit.md` for the tables, deletion test, witness ladder, and compression rules.

### Production Capacity Estimation: Causal Load Before Episode Structure

After the four-dimensional audit and continuous functional viewpoints pass, estimate episode capacity from **causal load**, never from unit count or raw scene count.

1. Keep three measures separate: continuous causal units (irreversible state changes), internal scene nodes (narrative actions), and minimum executable narrative beats (the smallest production-bearing actions that cannot be compressed without merging agency, evidence boundary, lived consequence, repayment, or the next debt).
2. For every unit, mark overlapping function counts: independent-life/agency, public consequence, evidence discovery/transfer/reinterpretation, conflict or irreplaceable choice, visible partial repayment, and end hook. Do not sum these counts into minutes; use them to detect density and high-risk crossings.
3. Mark production risk low/medium/high/very high. Protect false victory plus formal reversal; self-audit plus limited protection; public rule plus first real entry; separate self-exposures; external review plus responsibility splitting plus partial remedy; and mechanism formation plus immediate exclusion test.
4. Compare candidate episode counts using minimum beats per episode, scene nodes per episode, and whether the high-capacity turns retain separate execution. A season's late section may need more episodes than its raw scene count suggests because responsibility, evidence, witness, and public consequence cross there.
5. State a recommended candidate, a safe lower bound, and the condition required to expand higher. Do not expand merely by stretching phone calls, searches, notices, explanations, or meetings; that recreates the arranged, educational, filler feeling.
6. After capacity passes, make episode causal cards—not full scripts. Each card locks only inherited consequence, one emotional task, irreversible change, visible result, next missing answer, and AI execution boundary.

See `references/production-capacity-estimation.md` for the reusable table, risk definitions, protected nodes, and comparison method.

### Scope Gate: Season First, World Architecture Later

For a large ensemble project with a long-range organization, civilization, faction, or enterprise architecture, separate the work into two layers:

- **Current-season layer:** independent character lives, subjective successes, distinct consequences, causal encounters, the current gathering scale, the guide figure's present-stage action, and the season endpoint.
- **Deferred-architecture layer:** later enterprises, faction taxonomies, animal-core rosters, full organizational ecology, final core-member positions, late-season expansion, and civilizational mappings.

When the user explicitly says to improve the first season first, do not continue naming or classifying the deferred layer. Record the future direction as a short, clearly marked architecture note, then return to the current-season causal engine. Do not let a promising worldbuilding idea become a new active subplot.

Use this order:

1. Confirm what is being deferred and what remains active.
2. Mark deferred material as “架构存档/后续阶段，不进入当前季定稿”.
3. Lock the current season's endpoint and gathering scale before episode count.
4. Audit the existing independent-life cards against that endpoint.
5. Select one narrative anchor and derive the first encounter from a concrete consequence.
6. Only after that chain is approved, assign names, ages, locations, occupations, episode positions, or detailed faction identities.

A useful guardrail is:

> If a proposed detail does not change a current character's choice, risk, information, credit, resource, relationship, or season endpoint, defer it.

For this user's modern workplace Water-Margin ensemble, a valid early-season endpoint may be a one-person → two-person → four-person temporary community with a shared-venture seed, rather than all twelve characters meeting or the final enterprise being formally established. See `references/slow-gathering-ensemble.md` for the causality audit and independent-life-card gate, and `references/season-first-scope-gate.md` for the active-vs-deferred architecture checklist.

1. Lock the season's final public task, irreversible choice, and emotional/civilizational consequence before estimating episodes.
2. For an ensemble, prefer a public task that no single character can complete; define the task object, deadline, failure cost, and why each major character's contribution is structurally necessary.
3. Use a minimum as a floor, not a ceiling. If the user says “at least 30 episodes,” plan the first 30 as an initial phase and add episodes when causal turns, character debts, or the season endpoint need them. This user's hard rule for serial IP: **每季最少30集 / 不硬凑字 / 不缩减剧情 / 剧情不够就扩写**. When the approved causal units are fewer than 30, expand the plot by splitting each compressed unit's hidden causal steps into separate episodes — e.g. a one-line unit like “鼠牛各找一半事实” becomes 鼠查上游 / 牛查现场 / 拼出缺口 as three episodes — never by padding with repeated calls, searches, notices, or meetings. Before writing scripts, present a compact 扩写框架 (units → episodes mapping with per-episode 承接/情绪任务/扩写点/钩子) so the expansion direction is visible.
4. If the baseline is 3 minutes per episode, use it as the production default; do not stretch key episodes to 5 minutes merely to hit a runtime target.
5. Only after the endpoint and arc turns are locked should the episode index be built. The actual episode count is an output of causal load, not an input imposed on the plot.
6. A provisional scaffold such as five six-episode arcs may organize the first 30 episodes, but must not force a premature finale or compress unresolved consequences.

## Workflow

### Phase 1 — Audit Authoritative Material

Before inventing anything:

1. Find finalized character, world, relationship, and production documents.
2. Separate **fixed actor assets** from **this drama's fictional roles**.
3. List confirmed facts, proposed changes, unknowns, and conflicts.
4. Identify production constraints that change story design: visual medium, episode length, single-generation duration, child/multi-person/disaster difficulty, and target models.

Completion criterion: every important name, identity, relationship, timeline anchor, and production boundary is either confirmed or explicitly marked unknown.

### Phase 2 — Define Audience and Emotional Contract

Create a compact contract:

- Target audience;
- Familiar life wound;
- Emotional debt type;
- Fantasy or compensatory experience promised;
- Final repayment image;
- Character value that should remain after the爽点.

A genre promise may be familiar. The route to fulfillment must not be generic.

> Familiar emotional destination + unfamiliar causal route.

Completion criterion: the story can be summarized as “这个角色替哪群人，讨回哪一口气，以及为此失去或改变了什么”。

### Phase 3 — Build Character Agency

For each major character define:

- Want: what they consciously pursue;
- Fear: what loss they are avoiding;
- Blind spot: the self-serving belief that keeps them stuck;
- Leverage: ability, evidence, status, relationship, or secret;
- Choice: the decision only this character can make;
- Cost: what winning risks or sacrifices;
- Permanent change: what cannot return to the old state.

Rules:

- A gold finger may provide ability; the decisive choice must belong to the protagonist.
- A villain must damage something the audience values, not merely speak harshly.
- A sympathetic motive does not erase accountability.
- The protagonist should not win only because a father, husband, system, or powerful stranger arrives.

Completion criterion: remove each major character in turn; if the same plot still happens, that character lacks agency or function.

### Phase 4 — Draw the Information-Gap Table

Do not arrange episodes before tracking knowledge.

For each major character record:

| Character | Knows | Does not know | Misbelief | Therefore does |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Every reveal must answer:

- Who knew it first?
- How did they obtain it?
- Why did they act now rather than earlier?
- Who still does not know?
- What action becomes possible because of the new knowledge?

Avoid “剧情需要所以知道”. Separate evidence lines that serve different functions. An object, a manuscript, a witness, a timeline record, and formal verification should not all appear through one convenient event.

Detailed modern identity, hidden-information, and delayed-backfire patterns are in `references/information-gap-and-causality.md`.

### Phase 5 — Design the Season Repayment Schedule

Build the season before dialogue.

For each major arc specify:

- Debt borrowed;
- False relief;
- Escalation;
- Partial repayment;
- Counterattack;
- Public repayment;
- Permanent consequence;
- New debt or future promise.

Serial rule:

> Each episode may borrow a new debt, but it must repay or materially advance at least one existing debt.

Do not postpone every repayment to the finale. Repeated abuse without progress feels like secondary harm, not suspense.

Use `templates/season-blueprint.md`.

Completion criterion: no three consecutive episodes perform only setup, humiliation, or explanation without an irreversible change.

### Phase 6 — Build Episode Cards

Each episode card must contain:

1. Opening conflict or unfinished consequence;
2. One dominant emotional task;
3. Old debt advanced or repaid;
4. New information or changed relationship;
5. Protagonist action;
6. Concrete witnessed result;
7. End hook framed as a meaningful missing answer;
8. State change carried into the next episode.

The audience may already know the protagonist will win. Preserve **fulfillment-method suspense**:

- When will the debt be repaid?
- How will the protagonist do it?
- Who will witness it?
- What will the opponent lose or regret?
- What cost makes the victory belong to the protagonist?

Use `templates/episode-card.md`.

Completion criterion: the card can state what changed between the first and last beat without using “气氛更紧张” as the only change.

### Fast Workplace-Human-Conflict Opening (Short-video / AI Drama)

When the user wants a fast, human-nature-led workplace story—where the specific profession is not the attraction—load `references/workplace-human-conflict-opening.md` before writing or revising the opening episodes.

**Non-negotiable translation:** do not put an abstract workflow failure on screen and expect viewers to care. Translate it into an immediately legible human wound: a client the protagonist cultivated is handed to a connected newcomer; completed labor is paid less; a connected newcomer causes a problem and pushes an ordinary worker to sign for it.

For each opening episode, write and self-check this four-beat chain:

> visible unfairness at opening → costed choice → memorable action counterattack → partial outcome plus a specific next interpersonal debt.

The audience must be able to answer without industry knowledge: who was harmed, who benefited, what the protagonist risks by resisting, and who will bear the next cost. Professional processes, systems, contracts, and organizational background may only support a choice in one concise line; remove them if they obscure the human conflict.

Do not mistake a partial counterattack for a complete win. A good opening lets the protagonist gain one visible boundary or witness while losing status, income, control, or safety—and transfers a concrete consequence into the next episode.

### Phase 7 — Write Outline Before Script

Delivery order:

1. One-sentence premise;
2. Audience and emotional contract;
3. Character relationship map;
4. Information-gap table;
5. Season arc;
6. Episode cards;
7. Sample episode or full script only after outline approval.

Do not produce a full season of dialogue while identities, ages, evidence routes, or medium constraints remain unsettled.

### Phase 8 — Adapt to the Medium

The same emotional engine must be expressed differently:

- 爽文: long-term promise, growth system, and “苦难有偿化”; 
- 真人短剧: fast labels, visible conflict, frequent partial repayment;
- AI漫剧/动态漫: clear actions, rules, narration, sound, and visual symbols instead of subtle performance dependence;
- 一人分饰多角: precise social observation, fixed role relations, speech patterns, and recurring familiar-character chemistry.

See `references/medium-adaptation.md`.

### Phase 9 — Production Handoff

After the script is approved, hand off rather than duplicating downstream rules:

- Production-level shot design → `shot-language`;
- Character/scene/prop assets → image and scene skills;
- Seedance → `seedance-director`;
- LTX → `ltx-video-prompt`;
- MiniMax H3 or model-specific full-reference prompt → `prompt-master-pipeline` references.

Before handoff, mark scenes that are high-risk for AI generation: crowds, disasters, children, object handoffs, complex body contact, water, rapid costume/age changes, and multiple simultaneous speakers.

### Production-Constraint Layer for AI Ensemble Dramas

When episode causal cards are approved but before writing dialogue, shots, prompts, or assets, build a production-constraint table. It should lock only reusable functional locations, one visible action anchor per episode, on-screen character cap, evidence/prop continuity, interface-information cap, and how each high-risk action is split. This is especially required when the late season contains procedure, evidence, public remedy, or mechanism-building material: without it, the story can become meetings and screens even when its causality is sound.

Use `references/ai-ensemble-production-constraints.md` for the reusable location, P1–P4 character-density, I0–I3 interface, high-risk substitution, and season-end mechanism rules.

## Quality Gates

### Causality

- [ ] Every major action has motive, knowledge, opportunity, and consequence.
- [ ] Evidence enters through believable independent events.
- [ ] No reveal depends solely on coincidence or a suddenly appearing authority.
- [ ] Delayed consequences have a reason for the delay.

### Emotional Repayment

- [ ] The debt touches a recognizable value, not generic shouting.
- [ ] The protagonist acts rather than merely receives rescue.
- [ ] Repayment is visible to the people whose judgment matters.
- [ ] Victory carries cost, choice, or permanent change.
- [ ] Old debts are not postponed indefinitely.

### Serial Retention

- [ ] Each episode changes knowledge, leverage, relationship, or status.
- [ ] The end hook creates a specific missing answer.
- [ ] Reversals alter action; they are not decorative surprises.
- [ ] Quiet scenes still change trust, intention, or evidence.

### Character and IP

- [ ] The protagonist has a value beyond winning.
- [ ] The antagonist has a coherent fear or belief without being excused.
- [ ] Recurring relationships generate future stories.
- [ ] The finale resolves the season debt while leaving character life beyond the plot.

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Starting from a fashionable trope instead of an audience wound.** A trope is packaging; define the unresolved value underneath it.
2. **Making the grievance unbelievable.** Loud insults are not enough. Show loss of dignity, work, opportunity, love, truth, or autonomy.
3. **Endless oppression.** Repay small debts while larger ones grow.
4. **Arbitrary reversal.** A reversal without planted causality feels like author cheating.
5. **Private victory.** If the original humiliation was social, an unseen correction underpays the debt.
6. **Constant shouting.** Rhythm is pressure → rise → pause → eruption → fall → restart, not uninterrupted maximum intensity.
7. **Passive protagonist.** Power is not agency. The character must choose and act.
8. **One event proves everything.** Split identity, authorship, relationship, and legal/factual confirmation into separate evidence lines.
9. **Writing the whole season before locking constraints.** Confirm the framework first.
10. **Confusing viral beats with IP.** Conflict earns the click; relationship and growth earn return visits.
11. **Building on an obsolete authoritative doc.** When multiple episode-index or causal-chain drafts coexist, an older one may belong to an abandoned premise (e.g. a “跨组织交付结算” line vs the current “客户被抢→工资被砍→逼背锅” line). Verify which mainline is current before expanding or reusing an old episode count — do not silently proceed on a stale index whose opening episode no longer matches the current episode 1.
12. **Recall-style awakening is a punch-density bug.** 觉醒/翻盘流的爽点必须"现场炸裂"（时间停、敌人瞬间苍老、恶霸倒退回婴儿），不能写成"我当年一拳打穿盾"这种回忆交代。审查时逐章对照第一集力度，回忆式觉醒=漏点，要改成现场炸裂；逻辑漏洞"有神力却被困"可用"半觉醒"设定补（力量不稳、时有时无，觉醒过一次又脱力）。
13. **Planting a Chekhov's gun and never firing it.** 一个只出现一次就消失的能力/道具（如打火机），要么交代清楚来历（是谁递来的信物/为什么偏偏是它），要么给它后续用途。观众会一直挂念那个"凭空出现又消失"的东西——这就是 bug。
14. **Over-engineering rules.** 爽文规则要简单干净（"未来道具有时效、到点消失、借物耗力气"就够），不要加"越未来代价越大"这类拍脑袋补丁——用户会一眼看穿不合理。
15. **Ensemble IP: don't split the cast.** 生肖/动物群像IP，每个角色对应一类观众，冷落谁=放弃那类观众（足球队比喻：赢球人人有份，但每个位置都要有自己的高光）。群像爽点用"接力主角制"（每段一个主场，其余助攻+见证），而非平均分戏。

See `references/shuangwen-pacing-and-contrast.md` for 爽点节奏量化（前3秒钩子/憋屈≤30秒/落差即流量上限）、美强惨+越美越被当异类、动物特征反差、借物系统（道具时效+现代彩蛋+隐藏现代角色伏笔）、悖论死局设计。

12. **Over-engineering settings to paper over a plot hole (脑补过度加戏制造漏洞).** When the user points at a logical hole — e.g. "集齐十二神才能救龙，但龙自己就是十二神之一"、"打火机消失了，为什么火单独留下"、"越未来时效越短代价越大，这个设定不对吧" — do NOT layer a more complex setting on top to cover it. Two correct moves: (a) turn the hole INTO the plot — let a character raise the paradox on the spot, then resolve with a clean rule (方案试过才知道；留悬念、不一次成型；有阻碍；有人颓感但不表现出来); (b) simplify — the cleanest rule usually wins (未来道具有时效、到点消失；借物耗力气所以不能常借，这就够了，别叠加复杂度). Also: an ability/道具 that appears once then vanishes (未来道具只出现一次打火机) feels unresolved — make it recurring and diversified, each with a full borrow→use→expire→return loop.
12. **Self-referential “collect N to save Y” dead-loops.** When a premise is “gather all N members to save/restore Y”, first check whether Y is itself one of the N. If so (e.g. “gather the twelve time-gods to rescue the Dragon God” — but the Dragon is one of the twelve), the audience will spot the dead-loop instantly, and a sharp reader will name it before you do. Handle it as plot, never as an unnoticed hole: have a sharp character raise the paradox on the spot (“he’s one of the twelve — how do we ever complete the set?”), then design a way to break it (e.g. “his body is sealed, but his time-power still flows through time”). The paradox then becomes the villain’s trap and the protagonist’s central puzzle, not a bug. To keep tension, do NOT resolve it in one step — let the answer be guessed, doubted, and attempted-and-failed before it lands.

## Final Working Questions

Before approving any story, answer:

1. Who is this for?
2. What familiar emotional debt does it name?
3. Who or what rule created that debt?
4. Why is this protagonist qualified to confront it?
5. When does the audience first expect repayment?
6. How is repayment causal, concrete, public, and irreversible?
7. What permanent character change remains after the爽点?

Questions 1–6 decide whether the content can feel satisfying. Question 7 decides whether the character can become an IP.
