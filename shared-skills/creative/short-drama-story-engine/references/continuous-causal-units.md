# Continuous Causal Units Reference

适用于长篇群像、慢聚义、多线公共任务和需要延后估算集数的连续剧情。

## 1. Unit card

```text
Unit title:
Narrative block:
Opening state:
Primary emotional/material debt:
Evidence action:
Character with agency:
Choice and cost:
Visible result:
End-of-unit missing answer:
Clock movement:
Production-risk note:
```

A valid unit changes at least one state irreversibly: knowledge, risk, evidence, relationship, resource, status, or choice. Repeated argument, location change, or exposition is not enough.

## 2. Evidence boundary card

For every major evidence line, record:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| First appearance | Where does the evidence enter naturally? |
| Holder | Who possesses it first? |
| Transfer | Why does it move, and at what cost? |
| Proves | What conclusion can it support? |
| Cannot prove | What tempting conclusion must remain unresolved? |
| Reinterpretation | Which later fact changes its meaning? |
| Formal entry | When does it enter a public/institutional process? |

Do not let one object, log, witness, confession, or authority statement prove authorship, intent, contract responsibility, fairness, and final identity all at once.

## 3. Three-clock audit

For a public-task story, create one row per clock:

| Clock | First countdown | Local relief | Reverse acceleration | Concrete loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public/user continuity | When does an ordinary user lose service, income, safety, or access? | What temporary action buys time? | What new exclusion or failure reopens pressure? | What does the audience see? |
| Frontline survival | When do wages, debt, maintenance, equipment, credit, or eligibility deteriorate? | What partial payment or support appears? | Why can the frontline group no longer absorb delay? | Who pays in labor or money? |
| Review/legitimacy | When does a deadline, evidence window, or formal consequence start? | What creates a valid route to review? | What deadline or eligibility rule creates the next problem? | What status becomes irreversible? |

The clocks should cross. A plot where each clock runs in a separate phase often feels like three unrelated stories.

## 4. Slow-gathering entry audit

For every new character, answer:

1. What prior choice or consequence pulled them into the story?
2. Why are they affected now rather than earlier?
3. What do they know that the existing characters do not?
4. What do they still misunderstand or lack?
5. What do they want for themselves before helping anyone else?
6. What changes irreversibly after the encounter?

The first encounter should normally be transactional, suspicious, adversarial, or conditional. Relationship is produced by shared consequence, not declared in advance.

## 5. Capacity audit before episode count

Do not count units as episodes. Instead, annotate each unit with the number and type of internal beats it needs:

- independent-life/subjective-success beat;
- public-consequence beat;
- evidence-discovery beat;
- conflict/choice beat;
- visible result or partial repayment;
- end hook.

Then estimate production capacity from the completed unit cards. Some units may fit together; others—especially a false victory, collective self-exposure, public review, or mechanism formation—may need several episodes. Episode count is an output of causal load, not an input.

## 6. Three-unit continuity check

For every sliding group of three units, mark whether at least one of these changes:

- knowledge;
- risk;
- evidence;
- credit/reputation;
- resource;
- relationship;
- agency/choice.

If none changes, the sequence is likely padding. If the same change repeats without repayment or escalation, merge it or introduce a real consequence.

## 7. Common pitfalls

- Treating a narrative block as one episode by default;
- estimating a season from a legacy template before the causal chain is stable;
- compressing a collective choice into a speech or meeting;
- making a public clock abstract instead of showing ordinary loss;
- allowing a technical log to decide legal or moral responsibility by itself;
- letting a guide/institutional figure provide a condition and an outcome at once;
- ending with a vague “the group is formed” instead of a mechanism, cost, and next debt.
